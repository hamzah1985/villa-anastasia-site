About the Villa

Constructed in the year 2023 by local builder, Ton Korsrang Construction, this 3 bedroom 3 bathroom villa has a sunken sitting area, modern kitchen, tropical bath tub overlooking a peaceful garden, sala, private swimming pool and carport. This villa is a 5 minute walk to Makro and is only 3 km from the beach, providing comfort in luxury close to Aonang’s center.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and checkout times?

Check-in time is at 4:00 PM, and checkout time is at 11:00 AM.

Is the villa private?

Yes, it is a completely private standalone villa with its own pool and gated entrance.

Does every bedroom have a bathroom?

Yes, every room has its own dedicated bathroom for maximum comfort and privacy.

Is the villa near Ao Nang Beach?

Yes, we are conveniently located around 3 km away from the main Ao Nang Beach.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes, the layout, amenities, and location make it highly suitable for families and small groups traveling together.

Are there discounts for long stays?

Yes. Long-stay discounts are available in the low season, April to September. Choose your dates on Airbnb or Booking.com to see the rate, or message us there for a quote on a month or longer.

Are parties allowed?

No parties are allowed. We ask guests to respect quiet hours after 10:00 PM to maintain peace in the neighborhood.
